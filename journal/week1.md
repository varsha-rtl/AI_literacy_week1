# Week 1 - AI Engineering Journal

## What I Learned
This week I learned the difference between Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, and AI agents. I also learned how an LLM processes a prompt and generates a response. Another important learning was the difference between prediction, classification, and generation.

I also learned that AI is not always automatically correct and that verification is an important part of using AI responsibly.

## One AI Output I Initially Trusted

One AI-generated explanation that I initially trusted was an explanation of the difference between AI, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI. The answer was clear and sounded correct, so I initially accepted it.

## How I Verified It

I verified the important concepts by comparing the AI-generated explanation with reliable reference material. I also compared responses from ChatGPT and Gemini using the same prompt.

This showed me that I should check important claims instead of accepting an answer only because it sounds confident.

## What AI Did Well

AI helped me understand new concepts in a simple way and organize my answers. It was useful for explaining technical topics, providing examples, creating diagrams, and helping me structure my assessment.

## What AI Cannot Be Trusted to Decide

AI should not independently make important decisions involving health, law, finances, safety, or other high-impact situations. Human judgment and appropriate verification are necessary in these cases.

For engineering work, AI-generated code or technical suggestions should also be reviewed, tested, and verified before being used in a real system.

## One Question I Still Have
How can AI systems be designed to detect their own incorrect or unsupported answers before providing them to users?

## What I Will Do Differently Next Week

Next week, I will avoid accepting AI-generated information automatically. I will identify important claims, check reliable sources, and test technical outputs when possible.

I will use AI as a learning and productivity tool while taking responsibility for verifying the final information.
